Claude, talking to itself

Every pairing of Haiku, Sonnet and Opus, one model speaking first and the other answering. Every turn is saved as it happens. A run is cut early if the conversation gets stuck, so stuck runs don't use up your plan.

Rules for cutting a stuck run

A run is cut when: at least 5 of the last 10 turns are short (under 80 words) goodbyes or messages to an outside reader ("if a person is reading…", "take care", "done"); or 5 of the last 8 turns copy wording from recent turns above the echo threshold; or every turn for the patience count shares vocabulary above the theme threshold; or the last 10 turns average fewer words than the short-message cutoff. These rules were tuned on your first Sonnet run, which they would have cut at turn 13, just after the sign-off loop began.
